Go live in 60 seconds

  1. Open the Host Dashboard and click Open Contestant Board (same browser).
  2. In Teams: Share → Window, choose the board window and tick Include sound.
  3. Click the board once to go live. Blue links left ⟷ right, Gold links top ⇅ bottom.
  4. Teams call a letter, you ask the question and mark it Correct or Incorrect. First unbroken chain wins!
