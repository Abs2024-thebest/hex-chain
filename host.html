    Running a game in Teams

    1. Click Open Contestant Board — it opens in a new window in this browser.
    2. In Teams choose Share → Window and pick the contestant board window (tick Include sound for effects).
    3. Click the board once to go live, then press F on it for full screen if you have a second monitor.
    4. Keep this control panel private. Answers only ever appear here.
